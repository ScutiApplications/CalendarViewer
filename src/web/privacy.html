Privacy Policy

Philippines · Notice revision: 12 November 2026

CalendarViewer is local, read-only calendar software published under the developer name ScutiApplications. This notice explains this version’s behavior. The developer name is not a claim that a registered company exists.

Who handles your data

The standard app runs on your computer and listens on the local loopback interface. It does not send calendar contents to a ScutiApplications service. Publishing this software does not by itself establish that its developer controls your calendar processing. An employer, organization or other person operating a modified or hosted installation must identify their own role and provide appropriate notices.

Data used and why

  • Calendar labels, ICS links and imported files are used to display events, search, find overlaps and provide reminders. Links may contain access tokens. Events may contain names, descriptions, locations, meeting links and sensitive personal information.
  • Theme, views, working hours, refresh preferences and alert choices support features you choose. Browser caches keep imported calendars and feed snapshots available between visits.
  • If configured, AI provider settings and API keys are stored on your computer. Saved API keys are not returned to the browser. They are not encrypted by CalendarViewer; protect your Windows account and application folder.
  • Consent choices and the last displayed release version are stored in this browser. CalendarViewer has no built-in advertising or analytics tracking.

To provide background reminders, imported calendar content and alert settings can also be synchronized to this computer’s local server and stored in its private data directory. Notifications may display event details through your browser or Windows notification system, including on a lock screen if your system permits it.

Connections outside your computer

  • Adding or refreshing an ICS link contacts its calendar host from your computer. The host receives the request, your network address and credentials contained in the link. Meeting links you open are handled by their destination.
  • Automatic update checks contact GitHub when the page opens and every six hours while open. Installing an update requires your button press. GitHub receives ordinary connection metadata; the update request does not include your calendars or chat.
  • Downloading built-in models contacts GitHub, Hugging Face and Python runtime/package hosts. These hosts receive download requests and connection metadata. Installed Laya and MiniCPM inference runs locally; model setup does not require uploading calendars.
  • With your separate opt-in, hosted AI sends questions, recent chat context, working hours and calendar results requested by the model to the selected Anthropic, OpenAI or Google Gemini service. Results can include event titles, descriptions, locations and meeting links. Provider processing may occur outside the Philippines; provider terms and retention practices apply. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share.

API data policies: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini. Retention, model-training use and human review depend on the provider’s applicable API terms and your account configuration; consumer chat policies may differ. These links open external websites only when selected.

The local /mcp endpoint lets other programs on your computer query calendar data without a token. A connected client can read event details and may forward them elsewhere under its own configuration. CalendarViewer’s hosted-AI consent control covers its assistant routes; it does not control what separate MCP clients do. Keep the server on loopback and connect only trusted local programs.

Choices and consent

Reading this policy is not consent. Local calendar use does not require accepting hosted AI. Before hosted questions or connection tests, the app asks for a separate choice naming the provider. Choices apply to this browser and provider until withdrawn, browser data is cleared, or the notice revision changes. Use Calendar assistant settings → AI sharing consent to withdraw. Withdrawal stops this browser’s active answer and blocks future hosted requests; it cannot recall data already transmitted or withdraw choices made in other browser profiles.

Your choice does not provide consent on behalf of other people mentioned in a calendar. Organizations using the software must determine their lawful basis and additional safeguards, especially for sensitive personal information. Browser notification permission is a separate browser/operating-system control.

Storage, retention and removal

Browser calendar data and preferences generally remain until removed through the app or cleared in your browser. Server-side feed links, reminder state, settings, downloaded models and recovery backups reside under the app’s data/ folder, or its configured data directory. These files do not all expire when a calendar is removed. Exported backups and update rollback copies remain until you remove them.

Chat is held in page memory, cleared on reload, New chat, provider changes or withdrawal, and after five minutes without use once no answer is active. The app does not save a chat history file. Hosted providers may retain requests under their own policies. Setup/error and launcher logs can remain on disk; do not share logs without reviewing them.

Remove calendars and saved API keys through Settings where available. For complete local removal, stop CalendarViewer with run.bat stop, clear this site’s browser data, and remove its private data directory and any exported backups or rollback copies you no longer need. Removing model files requires downloading them again. Clearing browser storage alone does not remove server-side files. See Cookies & Local Storage.

Philippine privacy rights

Where the Philippine Data Privacy Act applies, rights include being informed, access, correction, objection, erasure or blocking, data portability and damages, subject to applicable conditions. You may raise a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. For data held by a calendar host, employer or AI provider, contact that responsible party; the developer cannot retrieve or erase copies it does not hold.

Contact and changes

For non-sensitive software questions, use the CalendarViewer repository. No dedicated private privacy email is currently published. Do not post calendar files, access tokens, API keys or private event details in public issues. Security vulnerabilities should use GitHub private vulnerability reporting where available.

This notice describes the standard local app and is not a compliance certification. A deployment collecting information for its operator needs its own accurate controller identity and contact arrangements. Material changes to hosted-AI disclosure require a fresh consent choice.
